# Citrus landing artwork

The active background is `public/citrus/orchard.webp` (1600 × 1000, approximately 316 KiB). It was created with the built-in ImageGen tool and converted to WebP at quality 88. The generated PNG remains outside the repository. The earlier photographic background and isolated raster fruit assets have been replaced; no unused versions are retained in the project.

The branch, leaves, arrow and small impact accents are native SVG in `src/landing/BotanicalScene.tsx`. The single featured fruit is owned by `ContinuousWheel` in `CitrusWheel.tsx`; `LandingPage.tsx` registers that same SVG to the scene camera and then its desktop anchor. There is no decorative duplicate or replacement wheel. The outer circle and outline remain the same node from whole orange to software wheel. `citrus-geometry.ts` supplies both the hand-drawn pulp paths and the working wheel paths; fixed point counts support continuous geometric interpolation. No cross-section image is substituted during the cut.

A single normalized controller applies the botanical camera and equivalent fruit pose, then hands that same fruit to normal desktop layout at a stateless, reversible threshold. Stable wheel-column flow prevents result panels from moving that threshold. The orange arrow travels on the fruit’s horizontal axis and is clipped as it reaches the trunk. All phases, including the small impact accents, are reversible functions of scroll progress. The workspace uses the same orange, cream, green, ink, outline thickness, and restrained SVG print pattern. System fonts and the existing Lucide icon set are retained.

## Final background generation prompt

Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: wide landscape background for a refined botanical editorial website, 1600x1000 composition. A lush orange tree viewed from below looking upward. Sophisticated hand-drawn 2D editorial illustration, organic irregular cut-paper foliage silhouettes with fine dark ink botanical vein linework, restrained risograph/woodcut print grain, limited flat colors ONLY soft sky blue #9ac9e5, leafy green #477456, deep green #234b36, citrus orange #ed8b31, warm cream #fff6e8 and dark green ink #233d2f. No photorealism, no 3D shading, no glossy rendering, no gradients. Approx 70 percent richly layered tree canopy and 30 percent open clear sky. Composition: richly detailed canopy across the TOP 25 percent and RIGHT 60 percent, small irregular groups of leaves framing bottom right. A broad dark green illustrated trunk curves from the right edge near bottom-right upward into branches near the top. One clear main trunk section passes through the far right at x=1450 y=450 in a 1600x1000 canvas. LEFT lower half is clean light blue sky for black headline, no lettering. Branches and leaves form natural irregular clusters, varied leaf shapes, delicate vein hatching, dark green limited shadow shapes. A few small whole oranges tucked among the upper and right leaves, all less than 70px diameter; no large hero fruit at center-right (one animated vector fruit will be added in code near x=1160 y=530). Leave a small patch of blue sky around x=1160 y=530 between foliage for that fruit. Warm airy daylight suggested with flat cream accents from upper left. Premium literary magazine illustration, elegant botanical study with confident graphic silhouettes. NO text, logos, arrows, knives, UI, frames, people.

## Playground fixtures

`sample-files.ts` creates valid, deterministic local sample bytes: a Canvas PNG illustration, a complete one-page PDF with xref table, UTF-8 Markdown and SRT, a short PCM WAV, a ZIP with a text entry, and CSV. These are selectable and downloadable files, not painted filenames. They do not trigger playback or execute code. No extra network requests, generated raster assets, or fonts are required.
